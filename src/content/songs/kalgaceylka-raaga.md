---
title: Kalgaceylka Raaga
title_en: The Love That Lingers
poet: Xussein Aw Faarax
singer: Xaliima Khaliif Magool
genre: Hees
decade: 1970s
themes: [Love, Longing]
somali: |
  Kalgaceylka raaga
  Toban sano halkuu rimo
  Wuu kureemay naafe

  Anna waxaan run iyo been
  Dadka raali galiyaba
  Waa ka ruugi aastee

  Raadkii ku soo noqoy
  Waana laba roogaalkii ×2
  Ribatayee allahayow

  Awrukba rarkiisi
  Kuma raran guubyoode
  Xarigii ruxmaa diila

  Geeluba ragaadkii
  Lugta uu kuriixe
  Ugu roortay qodhaxduye

  I rifyoo jaceylkii
  Wadnuhu ii riiqaye ×2
  Ribatayee Allahyow

  Ilma ragax tiraahdiyo
  Qalbigoo rugleeyiyo
  Naaskoo rafaadiyo

  Indha ruuxa dhawriyo
  Luga socod kuraagiyo
  Riyo kama gaboobe

  Rubbad uu jaceyl gale
  Raaxa kala daqaaqee
  Ribatayee allahayow
english: |
  The love that lingers
  Ten years in the place it took seed
  Has left me lame

  And I, with truth and with lies
  However I keep people content
  Chew it down and bury it

  It has come back on its own tracks
  And this is the second bout ×2
  I am trapped, O my God

  Even the pack camel's load
  Is not tied on carelessly
  The rope that slips leaves weals

  Among camels too, the lame one
  On the very leg it leans on
  The thorn has run in deepest

  Love has plucked me bare
  My heart has wasted away ×2
  I am trapped, O my God

  Tears that spill in a rush
  A heart that pounds
  A breast in torment

  Eyes that keep watch for one soul
  Legs that falter at walking
  And dreams I never outgrow

  A soul that love has entered
  Parts company with comfort
  I am trapped, O my God
---

The English is a first draft and is still being reviewed. Several lines are uncertain and may change.
